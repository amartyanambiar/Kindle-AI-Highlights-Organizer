**Stock Definition**
- Stock is a representation of a system's history of changing flows within it.

**Stock and Flow Concepts**

1. Stocks are a measure of how much of something is accumulated in a system at a given time. Examples include money in a bank account or inventory in a warehouse.

2. Flows represent the movement of resources into or out of a stock over a period of time. For example, money flowing in and out of a bank account through deposits and withdrawals.

3. A dam is often used as an analogy for understanding this concept: The water behind the dam (stock) is accumulated from the flow of water over time. The rate of water flow can change, affecting the level of the stock. (457-461)
